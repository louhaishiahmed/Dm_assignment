CAMPAIGN EDITORIAL

We Opened the Case.
It Was Water.

Campaign editorial · 2-minute read

The investigation

A silver briefcase. Two locks. Enough smoke to make an ordinary room look suspicious. Whatever was inside, someone clearly wanted us to take it seriously.

So we did. We studied the case. We watched the light spill across the metal. We considered diamonds, secret documents and something that probably required a password. Water was not our first guess.

The highly unnecessary reveal

Then the lid opened.

Inside stood a single can of Liquid Death mountain water. No diamonds. No secret documents. Just a drink with an entrance that belonged in an action film.

Was the security necessary? Absolutely not. Did we bring the smoke anyway? Obviously.

“THE CONTENTS WERE READY FOR A LUNCH BREAK.”

That is the idea behind “All That Drama. Just Water.” We took the simplest reveal imaginable and gave it an unreasonable amount of suspense. The case looked important. The lighting looked expensive. The contents were ready for a lunch break.

The joke is in the gap between what you expect and what you get. You spend a moment imagining something extraordinary, then discover that the entire operation was protecting a can of water.

Your statement

Now we need your version of events. Before the case opened, what did you think was inside? And what is your most dramatic reaction to being thirsty?

Maybe you announce it to everyone in the room. Maybe the walk to the fridge becomes an expedition. Either way, you understand the campaign.
